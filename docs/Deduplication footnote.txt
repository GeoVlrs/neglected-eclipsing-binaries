Prior to data acquisition, the output of the target selection script was inspected for duplicate entries originating from the source catalogue. The Kreiner (2004) ephemeris compilation includes multiple independent period solutions for a subset of systems, reflecting the fact that different authors published distinct timing analyses of the same star over the decades preceding the catalogue's assembly. When such entries cleared all three selection criteria independently, both appeared in the selected target list. Five systems were identified in this condition: EE Aqr, SX Crv, V839 Oph, V2150 Cyg, and SV Equ. In each case, the duplicate entry was resolved by retaining the period solution with the higher number of significant figures, on the grounds that a more precisely stated period reflects a more extensively timed system and therefore constitutes the more recently refined ephemeris. The less precise entry was removed, reducing the working sample from 399 to 394 unique targets before any photometric data were retrieved.
